Lucia, Pilar, and Mapi met in University. All from different backgrounds, brought together by one shared problem that arose in a class discussion about periods, women’s health, and nutrition. What started out as a group project to create an app that went beyond cycle tracking (since all the apps out there felt boring and bland), soon became our shared mission of making it our reality.

They noticed that many app recommendations on their socials didn't follow Gen Z trends, nor did they keep up with the current lifestyle goals and community first design that women on the internet have. So they set out to create an app that turns their cycle data into actionable, personalized nutrition, and movement guidance that adapts in real time to each and every woman’s biology and lifestyle, all while making it feel engaging, relevant and anything but boring.

Our core values

Preventative not Reactive

Understand biological warning signs before they become symptoms.

Personalized

Tailored to your individual 28-day biometric cycle, not statistical averages.

Evidence Based

Backed by peer-reviewed metabolic and endocrinological science.

Privacy by Design
