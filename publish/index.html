4 phases. 1 body.

Your body cycles through different hormonal needs everyday. NutriSync reads where you are across your cycle and adapts food and movement.

Menstrual

Estrogen and progesterone drop. Your body is resting, healing, and reflecting. Slow down.

Follicular

Estrogen levels climb, sparking high mental clarity, creative focus, and physical stamina.

Ovulatory

Estrogen peaks alongside testosterone. You feel highly social, confident, and physically resilient.

Luteal

Progesterone dominates before dropping. Your energy winds downward. Prioritize self-comfort.
