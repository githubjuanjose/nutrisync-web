2 · Notas de la reunión 1 — lo importante

Tal como las escribió Juanjo tras la llamada (en inglés, sin editar). Lo que Tony afirma sobre sí mismo sigue sin verificar y el paquete no lo usa como aval.

New, specific credential he didn't have before this call: he described a former Action Ventures partner who ran GE Healthcare's health division (3,000 employees, 15 health hubs across the US, Canada, Europe, Asia), who personally selected the health-sector spin-offs the fund invested in — 55 investments over 15 years, only 3 failures by his account. He also mentioned buying medtech spin-offs from Stryker, Meditech, and Philips. That's the first concrete, sector-relevant claim we've seen from him — still unverified, but no longer nothing.

Direct feedback on your deck (this is the valuable part): he said the team and market-size framing work well, but two things are missing — a practical go-to-market section ("this is our reasonable share of the market, and this is how we take it," not just TAM/SAM/SOM), and a clear ROI promise for investors, which you have in your spreadsheet but not in the deck itself. He also noted European decks can state ROI directly, unlike the US post-2008.

The ask conversation, which is the headline: his circle had already asked him "why is Pilar asking so little money." He pushed you twice to raise more — asking if €2M or €5M would let you accelerate faster (you said yes), then assigning homework: think concretely about what that bigger number would actually let you do. He said he has "a trick" for valuation and wants to discuss investment instruments next call. He gave no personal check size and no commitment — his diligence process takes 6–10 weeks.

One thing worth flagging to yourself: live on the call you told him "~€30K to launch, operations only" — a third number alongside the deck's €350K and the financing document's €15–25K. It's close enough to your €24,915 target bridge that it's probably the same figure rounded, but align it explicitly before it comes up again.

Does he sound like he wants to invest big? Genuinely warmer than an average first call. Specific, costly pitch feedback and an unprompted, twice-repeated push toward a much bigger number are real signals — people who don't intend to engage don't usually bother. But it's fair to hold two things at once: his advice to raise more may be sound, and his own business (advisory fees, a bigger deal for his investor club) benefits from you raising more too. Nothing here is a commitment yet — no check size, no term sheet. Call two, when valuation and deal structure are actually on the table, is the real test.

5 · Posición propuesta para la reunión (del paquete, §20)

NutriSync is a founder-led, cloud-delivered business. We intend to move ahead with our own capabilities and a controlled operating plan. The €15–25K level covers narrowly scoped near-term work; €250–350K supports launch and early validation. A €2M plan would build repeatable execution in Spain, while €5M would allow more parallel product, expert-content, distribution and marketing work, plus selected international readiness and pilots. We are exploring Australia and Singapore as well as European opportunities. We will not count grants, partnerships or investment until the relevant commitments are real. The next decision is which scope can be justified by evidence, capacity and acceptable terms — not simply how much capital is available.

Requiere la aprobación de las founders antes de circular como posición formal de la empresa.
