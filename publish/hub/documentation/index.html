Proposal 33 — Wearables (Epic O) · ES/EN
5 Aug: connect NutriSync to Apple Health and Health Connect — what we read and why, what it feeds, and what we must not do. Two findings that change the plan: Fitbit's Web API shuts down in September 2026, and Strava's agreement forbids both AI training and showing a user's data to anyone else. The governing rule: objective data fills fields, subjective states stay hers and become the axis that explains patterns — mood is never inferred from a watch. Five decisions for founders (D15–D19). Language switch top-right.
